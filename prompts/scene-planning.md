# Scene-planning prompt
Turn the approved script into `scenes.json`: one entry per beat with start/end (from the real voiceover timing), visual action, reusable components used, on-screen text, and sound notes. Each scene must pass the scene test (new fact, stakes, mechanism, expectation change, evidence, consequence, or climax).

Brand rules: on-screen words are a Title (Abril Fatface) or a Subtitle (Inter) only. Never use eyebrow/kicker labels (small tracked uppercase text). Flow/step diagrams use tall vertical glass cards (`GlassCard`).
