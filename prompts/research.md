# Research prompt
Build `research.md` and `sources.json` for the episode topic in `brief.md`.
- Follow the research hierarchy (primary documents, quality reporting, academic reviews, books, forums only as leads).
- Source ledger per claim: claim, exact source, date, excerpt/page, primary or secondary, confidence (high/medium/disputed), caveats.
- Never invent dialogue, motives or scenes. Flag contested explanations.
