# Episode 001 brief

- **Topic (idea bank #18):** Why a $9.99 price feels different from $10
- **Purpose:** Quick, easy-going test video to see whether this content type performs. Low production risk, high shareability.
- **Series:** C, The Mind's Loopholes
- **Central question:** Why does one cent make a price feel so much cheaper?
- **Hidden mechanism:** The left-digit effect. The first digit sets the perceived magnitude, so 9.99 is read nearer 9 than 10.
- **Length:** about 2 minutes (script 287 words)
- **Format:** 16:9 master. Open question: also produce a vertical 9:16 cut for Shorts/Reels/TikTok?
