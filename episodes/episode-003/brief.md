# Episode 003 brief
Topic: Endowment effect. Why owning something raises its price in your head.
Format: vertical short, ~75 s. Channel: AhadInsights.
Core claim: in Kahneman, Knetsch & Thaler (1990), sellers valued identical mugs at more than twice what buyers would pay, and trade volume was far below prediction.
Honesty notes: say "in one run/experiment"; effect size varies by study; some critics (e.g. Plott & Zeiler 2005) argue design, not loss aversion, drives part of the gap.
