## Description
Students were given a coffee mug, and suddenly it was worth more than twice what a buyer would pay. Here is the endowment effect in 78 seconds.
Based on Kahneman, Knetsch & Thaler (1990), "Experimental Tests of the Endowment Effect and the Coase Theorem", Journal of Political Economy.
